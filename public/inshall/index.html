Curio

A personal inspiration library, made by Inshall for his own use. Not for sale, no ads, no other users.

Curio brings the things I save — YouTube videos, Pinterest pins, posts and projects from other sites — into one calm place on my own computer and on my own private web page, next to my notes, tasks, documents, calendar and personal finance. It helps me find, sort and use my own saved inspiration for my design work and personal growth.

Who it is for

Only me, Inshall, an individual designer and developer in Karachi, Pakistan. Curio is a personal project for my own development and learning. It is not offered to the public and is not sold. The web version only lets one Google account sign in: mine.

What it does

  • Library and Moodboard — my own saves in one place, with my own boards and notes.
  • YouTube — reads my own playlists through the official YouTube Data API, so I can watch and find my saved videos.
  • Pinterest — reads my own boards and pins through the official Pinterest API, so my pins sit next to my other saves.
  • Workspace — my tracker, documents, calendar and personal finance.

Under development after API approval

Organize — one screen to tidy my own YouTube playlists and Pinterest boards from inside Curio:

  • make, rename and reorder my own playlists and boards;
  • move my own saved videos and pins between them, several at once, by drag and drop;
  • see my saves by month.

Every change goes through the official API, only on my own account, only when I press a button. Curio never posts, likes, follows, comments or touches anyone else's content.
